## Description Draft

AURA is an AI personal stylist for your real closet. Add clothing from photos, clean up item images, organize your wardrobe, and chat with AURA for outfit ideas that use what you already own.

Plan looks for today, save outfits, import product links into closet drafts, and use weather or calendar context when you choose to grant those permissions. AURA is currently in beta, so AI suggestions and product metadata may be imperfect. Please use your own judgment before wearing, buying, or relying on any recommendation.
